**20 executable Claude skills based on Claude Hopkins' *Scientific Advertising* (1923).**

Copy audits · Headline workshops · Offer design · Campaign diagnostics · 30-day launch plan — every rule traceable to a specific Hopkins chapter.

---

## What This Is

Claude Hopkins wrote *Scientific Advertising* in 1923. David Ogilvy called it required reading. The rules inside — service-first framing, qualifying headlines, specific claims over generalities, approval offers, keyed measurement — remain the most reliable framework for direct-response advertising ever written.

This plugin operationalises the entire book as 20 Claude skills. Each skill is a single SKILL.md file you load into a Claude conversation. Give Claude your product, your copy, or your campaign data. Get back a specific, structured output grounded in Hopkins' original principles.

No generic marketing advice. No code. No APIs. No installation. Load a file, describe your business, get a result.

## Free Starter Pack (5 Skills)

These five skills are available in this repo at no cost. They cover a complete creative arc: audit what you have, fix the framing, write better headlines, build curiosity hooks — then use the onboarding skill to see exactly which paid skills your business needs next.

### Skill #2 — Copy Audit
`Skills/02-copy-audit/SKILL.md`

Line-by-line Hopkins review of any ad, landing page, or email. Returns a three-tier score (PASS / NEAR-PASS / FAIL) for each element with a specific Hopkins principle behind every verdict. False-positive resistant — strong copy scores PASS, not FAIL because something could theoretically be sharper.

### Skill #4 — Headline Workshop
`Skills/04-headline-workshop/SKILL.md`

Generates qualifying, desire-based headlines using Hopkins' rules: select the right reader, not the largest audience; lead with a specific benefit, not a clever turn of phrase; make the headline earn a click from your buyer, not from everyone. Returns multiple variants with principle-by-principle rationale.

### Skill #5 — Service-Frame Rewriter
`Skills/05-service-frame-rewriter/SKILL.md`

Takes brand-cry copy ("We are the leading provider of...") and rewrites it as service-first copy that leads with what the reader gets, not what the business is. Diagnoses how many lines are business-framed vs. reader-framed, then rewrites each one. Pairs naturally with Copy Audit output.

### Skill #7 — Curiosity Hook Builder
`Skills/07-curiosity-hook-builder/SKILL.md`

Excavates the hidden, surprising facts inside any product — process steps, ingredient mechanics, overlooked specifics — and turns them into curiosity-driven headlines and hooks. Three-pass discovery: process mining, ingredient inspection, overlooked obvious. Output is immediately deployable in ad copy or social content.

### Skill #18 — Plugin Onboarding
`Skills/18-plugin-onboarding/SKILL.md`

Diagnoses your situation (fresh start / copy but no campaign / campaign running well / campaign running poorly) and produces a personalised roadmap — which skills to run, in which order, and what to bring to each. Best used after trying the four skills above: you'll see exactly which of the 15 remaining paid skills your business needs, and why.

## How to Use Any Skill

1. Open the SKILL.md file in this repo (or from the paid bundle)
2. Copy the full contents
3. Paste it as the system prompt in a new Claude conversation
4. Describe your product, business, or paste your existing copy
5. Read the output — every finding traces to a Hopkins quote

**Recommended model:** Claude Sonnet or Opus. Haiku may miss nuance on three-tier scoring judgments.

## Quality Standard

Every skill in this plugin was built to the same bar:

- Manual run completed on a real business before the skill was written
- 5 eval cases per skill: weak copy fires correctly; strong copy does not get penalized
- Three-tier scoring (PASS / NEAR-PASS / FAIL) — no binary verdicts that punish nuance
- Every rule traces to a specific Hopkins chapter and quote — no inventions, no additions

## Philosophy

A skill file, once built, costs nothing to distribute and nothing to use. You load it once. It applies the same discipline to your tenth campaign as it did to your first. There is no ongoing subscription to a methodology that hasn't changed since 1923.

This is not a new theory of advertising. Hopkins already did that work. This plugin removes the gap between *knowing* his principles and *applying* them systematically to a live product. The authority here is Hopkins — not the creator of this plugin.

Every rule is cited. Every citation is verifiable. If you have *Scientific Advertising* open in one window and a skill running in another, you can audit the skill against the source. That traceability is the product.
