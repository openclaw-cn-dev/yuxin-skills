
# Cold Outreach Copy (开发信)

## Goal

A short, personalized email sequence that a busy overseas buyer will read, understand in ten seconds and want to answer.

## Before you start: gather context

- What the user sells and to whom (importers, distributors, retailers, brands, agencies, partners)
- The single most relevant result or proof (clients, numbers, certifications, MOQ, lead time, samples)
- Prospect list fields available: name, company, role, website, recent news, products they carry
- The desired next step: reply, sample request, short call, catalog request

## Research and personalization

For each prospect, find one specific, true reason for reaching out:
- A product line they carry that fits
- Something new: expansion, new store, new product launch, hiring
- A gap visible on their site or in their reviews
Write a one-line personalized opener from this. If there is nothing specific, segment by industry and personalize at the segment level rather than faking it.

## Email structure (50–125 words)

1. **Subject**: short, plain, specific (2–5 words), looks like an internal email, no hype or ALL CAPS
2. **Opener**: about them, not about you
3. **Relevance**: the problem you solve for companies like theirs
4. **Proof**: one concrete result or credential
5. **CTA**: one low-effort question ("Worth sending a sample?" / "Open to a 10-minute call next week?")
6. **Signature**: name, role, company, website; plus the opt-out line and address required by law (see `references/cold-email-setup.md`)

## Follow-up sequence

4–6 touches over about three weeks, each adding something new:

| Touch | Day | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Personalized first email |
| 2 | 3 | Short bump with one new detail or proof |
| 3 | 7 | Case study or relevant example |
| 4 | 12 | Different angle: cost, speed, risk reduction, sample offer |
| 5 | 18 | Simple question: "Who handles sourcing for X?" |
| 6 | 25 | Polite close-the-loop |

Reply in the same thread; stop the sequence as soon as they reply or opt out.

## Rewriting typical exporter emails

Avoid:
- "Dear Sir/Madam" → use their first name
- "We are a professional manufacturer with 20 years of experience" → state the result buyers get
- Full catalog attachments and many links in the first email → offer to send the catalog if interested
- Long paragraphs listing every product → pick the one product line that fits them
- Over-formal, translated phrasing → plain, friendly business English

## Handling replies

- Interested: answer questions directly, propose a specific next step
- Not now: ask permission to follow up in a set number of months
- Wrong person: thank them and ask for the right contact
- Unsubscribe: remove immediately, confirm briefly

## Rules of thumb

- Never fake familiarity ("as we discussed"), fake reply threads ("Re:" when there was no prior email) or false claims.
- One email, one ask.
- Read every email out loud; if it sounds like a template, rewrite it.

## Output format

1. Personalization variables to collect for each prospect
2. Five subject line options
3. Full sequence (all touches) with personalization placeholders
4. Reply-handling templates
