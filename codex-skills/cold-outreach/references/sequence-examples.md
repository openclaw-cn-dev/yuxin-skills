# Outreach Sequence Examples

## Example A: Manufacturer → US retailer (开发信)

Fictional sender: Lily at **Brightwell Drinkware**, a stainless-steel tumbler manufacturer. Prospect: Megan, buyer at a regional gift-store chain that already sells insulated tumblers.

Personalization variables: `{first_name}`, `{company}`, `{observed_detail}` (for example a product line they carry), `{relevant_result}`.

**Email 1 (Day 0)**
Subject: tumblers for {company}

> Hi Megan,
>
> Saw {company} just added a summer drinkware table in your Asheville store. Nice mix of colors.
>
> We make insulated tumblers for boutique retailers, with custom colors from 300 units and US warehouse stock, so reorders arrive in about a week instead of a month.
>
> A gift shop chain in Ohio added our 20 oz line last spring and reordered three times by August.
>
> Want me to send two samples in your best-selling colors?
>
> Lily
> Brightwell Drinkware · brightwelldrinkware.com
> [Postal address] · Reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.

**Email 2 (Day 3)**: same thread
> Hi Megan, one detail I left out: we can print your store logo on the base, which a few retailers use for local souvenir lines. Happy to include a logo sample too.

**Email 3 (Day 7)**
> Megan, here's a one-page sheet on how the Ohio store set up their tumbler display and what sold first: [link]. Might be useful even if we don't work together.

**Email 4 (Day 12)**: different angle
> Hi Megan, a lot of buyers tell me their pain is stockouts mid-season. Because we hold US stock, the smallest reorder is 50 units and ships in 5–7 days. Would that help with your fall/holiday planning?

**Email 5 (Day 18)**
> Quick one: are you the right person for drinkware, or is there someone else I should talk to?

**Email 6 (Day 25)**: close the loop
> Hi Megan, I'll stop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If drinkware comes up for next season, I'm one reply away.

## Example B: Brand → US agency partnership

Subject: idea for {agency}'s DTC clients

> Hi {first_name},
>
> Noticed {agency} runs paid social for several home and wellness brands.
>
> We're a seat-cushion brand with a 30-day guarantee and a creator program, and we're looking for one agency to test a revenue-share partnership on retargeting creative.
>
> Open to a 15-minute call next week to see if it's a fit?

## Subject line options

- {company} + drinkware
- quick question, {first_name}
- samples for {company}?
- idea for your summer table
- reorders in 7 days

## Reply handling templates

**Interested**
> Great, thanks Megan! Which two colors sell best for you? I'll ship samples to the store address on your site unless you'd prefer another one.

**Not now**
> Totally understand. Mind if I check back in {month} before holiday buying starts?

**Wrong person**
> Thanks for letting me know. Who would be the best person for drinkware buying?

**Unsubscribe**
> Understood. I've removed you and won't email again.

All names, companies and results above are fictional placeholders.
