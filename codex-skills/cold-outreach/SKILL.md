## Core rules

- Never send cold email from the main company domain.
- Warm up new mailboxes for 2–4 weeks; scale by adding mailboxes, not by pushing one harder.
- Verify every address; keep bounces under about 2% and complaints near zero.
- 50–125 words, opener about them, one proof, one low-effort question.
- Include a postal address and a clear opt-out; honor opt-outs immediately.
- No fake "Re:" threads, fake familiarity or false claims.
