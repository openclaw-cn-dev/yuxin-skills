# Citation Tier Reference

Source tiers used to label every claim and reference in the RAS cold-water
aquaculture skill. Mirrors `SECOND-KNOWLEDGE-BRAIN.md` ?1.2.

| Tier | Definition | Examples |
|------|------------|----------|
| 1 | Systematic review / meta-analysis / official standard | FAO RAS guidelines, WOAH Aquatic Animal Health Code, APHA Standard Methods, ISO standards |
| 2 | Peer-reviewed academic paper / RCT | Emerson et al. 1975 (10.1139/f75-274); Benson & Krause 1984 (10.4319/lo.1984.29.3.0620); Zhu & Chen 2002 (10.1016/S0044-8486(02)00051-8); Eding et al. 2006 (10.1016/j.aquaeng.2005.09.003) |
| 3 | Industry report / professional association guideline | SRAC factsheets, Freshwater Institute RAS bulletins, USDA APHIS aquaculture guidance |
| 4 | News / blog / vendor material | YSI/Hach sensor application notes, trade press, vendor whitepapers |

## Authoritative domain sources (canonical registry)

- **FAO Aquaculture Department** ? fao.org/fishery (RAS & water-quality guidelines, cultured species factsheets) ? Tier 1
- **DFO Canada (Fisheries & Oceans)** ? dfo-mpo.gc.ca (cold-water salmonid/char culture) ? Tier 1/3
- **The Freshwater Institute** ? freshwaterinstitute.org (RAS research, biofilter & water reuse) ? Tier 3
- **SRAC / US Regional Aquaculture Centers** ? extension factsheets (water-quality tables) ? Tier 3
- **World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH)** ? woah.org (Aquatic Animal Health Code & notifiable diseases) ? Tier 1
- **USDA APHIS Aquaculture** ? aphis.usda.gov (disease surveillance) ? Tier 3
- **US EPA** ? Water Quality Criteria (ammonia criteria for aquatic life) ? Tier 1
- **NOAA Fisheries Aquaculture** ? fisheries.noaa.gov (aquaculture regulations & health) ? Tier 1/3
