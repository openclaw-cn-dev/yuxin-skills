# Lead Qualification (客户筛选)

Goal: spend time on leads most likely to buy, give everyone a fast and polite answer, and route the rest to automated nurture.

## Simple scoring (0–10)

Score each lead on four questions. Adjust weights to the business.

| Factor | 0 | 1 | 2–3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fit**: are they the right customer? | Wrong market / can't use product | Partly fits | Clear fit (right country, use case, business type) |
| **Need**: is the problem real and specific? | Just browsing | General interest | Specific problem or project |
| **Budget / quantity** | Far below price or MOQ | Close | Matches price or MOQ |
| **Timing** | No timeline | Within 3 months | Within a month / urgent |

Max 10 (Fit 3 + Need 2 + Budget 3 + Timing 2, or similar).

| Score | Temperature | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 8–10 | Hot | Reply personally now; offer a call, quote or checkout link today |
| 5–7 | Warm | Personal reply within hours; send proof, sample offer or FAQ; follow up in 2–3 days |
| 0–4 | Cold | Polite helpful answer, add to email nurture, no chasing |

## Qualifying questions

Ask two or three, one per message. Never interrogate.

**B2C**
- What are you hoping it'll help with?
- Is this for you or a gift?
- Any size, color or fit concerns?
- When do you need it by?

**B2B**
- What kind of store or business do you run?
- Which products are you looking at, and roughly what quantity?
- When would you need the first order delivered?
- Do you need custom branding or packaging?
- Who else is involved in the decision?

## Signals

**Buying signals**: asks about delivery date, payment methods, bulk pricing, samples, customization, return policy details; mentions a deadline or event.

**Warning signals**: asks only for the lowest price with no other context; wants large free samples with no company details; requests payment to unusual accounts or offers overpayment refunds (common scam pattern); refuses to share any business information for a wholesale quote.

## Polite disqualification

> Thanks so much for asking! Our minimum order is 300 units, so we may not be the best fit for 50 pieces right now. If your volume grows, I'd love to help. Here's our retail store in case it's useful: [link]

> Thanks for reaching out! We currently only ship within the US and Canada, so I can't help with delivery to {country} yet. I'll let you know if that changes.

## Response time targets

| Temperature | First reply | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Hot | Under 15 minutes in business hours | Same day |
| Warm | Under 2 hours | 2–3 days later |
| Cold | Within 1 business day | Email nurture only |

## Weekly pipeline check

- New leads by source and score
- Reply time (median)
- Conversion rate by stage (contacted → qualified → offer → won)
- Top 3 lost reasons
- Sources that bring hot leads → tell the ads side to buy more of those (`paid-ads`, `ads-optimization`)
