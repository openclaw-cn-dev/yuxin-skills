# FAQ Library (常见问题)

One FAQ powers four places: chat replies, the landing page FAQ, email flows, and any chatbot or AI assistant. Keep each answer short, specific and true.

## Building the FAQ

1. Export the last 50–100 chats and emails.
2. Group questions by topic; count how often each appears.
3. Write one clear answer per question (1–3 sentences, plain English).
4. Add a "short chat version" for quick replies.
5. Review monthly: add new questions, fix answers when policies change.
6. Send the top objections to the ads side; they make strong ad angles (`ad-copy`).

## B2C categories and example answers (fictional CoreSeat)

**Shipping**
- *How long does shipping take?* Orders ship from our US warehouse in 1–2 business days and usually arrive in 3–5 business days. You'll get tracking by email.
- *Do you ship internationally?* We currently ship to the US and Canada.

**Returns & guarantee**
- *What if it doesn't work for me?* Try it for 30 days. If it's not more comfortable, contact us for a return.
- *Who pays return shipping?* State the real policy clearly.

**Product**
- *Will it fit my chair?* It's {dimensions} and fits most office and car seats.
- *Is it firm or soft?* Medium-firm: soft enough to sink in, firm enough to support.
- *How do I clean it?* Remove the cover and machine wash cold; air-dry the foam if needed.
- *Will it go flat?* Explain the material simply, with real long-term reviews if available.

**Payment & trust**
- *What payment methods do you accept?* List the real options (cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Shop Pay).
- *Is my payment secure?* Checkout is handled by {platform}; we never see your full card details.
- *How do I contact you?* Email, chat hours and time zone.

**Orders**
- *Can I change or cancel my order?* Explain the window (for example within 12 hours, before it ships).
- *Do you offer discounts?* Explain the honest policy (welcome code, bundles, seasonal sales).

## B2B categories and example answers (fictional Brightwell Drinkware)

**Ordering**
- *What's your MOQ?* 300 units per design; colors can be split within one order.
- *Can I get samples?* Yes, 2 pieces; the sample fee is credited on your first order.
- *Do you offer custom branding?* Laser engraving and printed logos; custom packaging from 1,000 units.

**Production & delivery**
- *What's the lead time?* About 25 days for production plus shipping time; reorders from US stock ship in 5–7 days.
- *Which incoterms do you offer?* FOB and DDP to the US (state only what you actually offer).

**Quality & compliance**
- *Which certifications do you have?* List only real, current certificates and offer copies.
- *What if items arrive damaged?* Explain the claim process and timeline.

**Payment**
- *What are your payment terms?* State the real terms (for example 30% deposit, 70% before shipping) and accepted methods.

## Chatbot / AI assistant notes

If the FAQ feeds a chatbot:
- Give it only verified answers from this library.
- Tell it to hand off to a human for refunds, complaints, custom quotes and anything not in the FAQ.
- Never let it invent policies, discounts or delivery dates.
