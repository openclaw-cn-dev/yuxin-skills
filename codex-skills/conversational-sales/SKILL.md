## Core rules

- Speed wins: reply to new inquiries within minutes during business hours in the customer's time zone; set an instant auto-reply outside them.
- Every lead gets registered and tagged the same day; no lead lives only in someone's chat app.
- Qualify before you pitch: ask two or three questions, then recommend one option.
- Native, friendly English: short messages, one question at a time, no walls of text, no "Dear Sir".
- Honest and low-pressure: no fake scarcity, no invented reviews, no promises the business can't keep.
- Consent matters: message only people who contacted you or opted in; follow each platform's messaging rules (for example WhatsApp Business limits on messaging outside the customer-service window). Confirm current rules.
- Turn every repeated question into an FAQ entry, and every repeated objection into ad copy or page content.
