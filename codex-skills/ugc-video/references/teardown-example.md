# Teardown Example

Illustrative teardown of a fictional 24-second UGC ad for a kitchen gadget (an oil splatter guard). Use the same format for real videos.

## 1. Hook verdict

- **Conflict present?** Yes: person vs problem. Oil spits onto the creator's arm in frame 1 and she flinches.
- **Score**: 4/5. Strong visual conflict, but the product doesn't appear until 6 s; showing it by 3 s would tighten it.

## 2. Timeline breakdown

| Time | Shot / visual | Camera | Spoken line | On-screen text | Sound | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 s | Oil spits from pan onto forearm, creator flinches | Close-up, handheld | "Ow—every time." | "Every. Single. Time." | Sizzle, gasp | Hook / conflict |
| 1.0–3.0 s | Grease spots across stovetop and backsplash | Wide pan | "And then I'm scrubbing for 20 minutes." | "20 min of cleanup 🙃" | Sizzle | Escalation |
| 3.0–6.0 s | Creator rolls eyes, reaches off-screen | Medium | "My sister sent me this." | — | Light music starts | Transition |
| 6.0–12.0 s | Guard placed over pan, steam passes, no spatter | Overhead | "Steam gets out, grease doesn't." | "Steam out. Grease in." | Sizzle quieter | Demo |
| 12.0–17.0 s | Wipes clean stovetop with one swipe | Close-up | "Cleanup is literally this." | "1 swipe" | — | Proof |
| 17.0–21.0 s | Folds guard flat, slides into drawer | Close-up | "And it folds flat." | "Fits in a drawer" | — | Objection buster (storage) |
| 21.0–24.0 s | Holds product to camera, points down | Medium | "Link's below, it's on sale this week." | "Sale ends Sunday" | Music up | CTA |

## 3. Pattern summary

- **Structure**: conflict → escalation → reveal → demo → proof → objection → CTA
- **Pacing**: 7 cuts in 24 s, average shot ~3.4 s; fastest cuts in the first 3 s
- **Retention devices**: physical pain in frame 1 (instant empathy), "my sister sent me this" (social trust + curiosity), visual payoff at 6 s
- **Proof**: visual demo instead of claims
- **Weak points**: product reveal late (6 s); CTA uses urgency ("sale ends Sunday"), which must be real; no price shown

## 4. New scripts (pattern reused, content original)

Example for a different product (fictional CoreSeat cushion):

| Time | Visual | Spoken line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 s | Creator stands up from desk chair and freezes mid-rise, hand on lower back | "Every. Single. Afternoon." | "3 pm back check ✋" |
| 1.5–3 s | Quick shot of CoreSeat already on the chair | "Until this." | "This thing →" |
| 3–9 s | Hand presses foam, it recovers | "My old pad was flat in a month. This bounces back." | "Doesn't go flat" |
| 9–15 s | Time-lapse of a workday, creator sitting relaxed | "Full day of calls." | "9 am → 6 pm" |
| 15–19 s | Stands up easily, no groan | "And I just… stand up." | "No groan 🙌" |
| 19–22 s | Unzips and tosses cover in the washer | "Cover goes in the wash." | "Washable" |
| 22–25 s | Points down | "30 days to try it. Link below." | "30-day guarantee" |

Alternative conflict hooks:
1. Partner off-camera: "Why do you groan every time you stand up?"
2. Old cushion pressed flat: "Four weeks. That's all it lasted."
3. Car clock at 2 hours: "Back's done and we're halfway there."
