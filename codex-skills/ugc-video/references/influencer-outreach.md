
# Influencer & Creator Outreach

## Goal

A repeatable creator program: the right creators found and vetted, clear offers, smooth briefs, content the brand can also use in ads, and proper disclosure.

## Before you start: gather context

- Product, price, margin, sample cost
- Goal: sales, content for ads, awareness, reviews
- Platforms and target audience (country, age)
- Budget and preferred deal type

## Creator tiers

| Tier | Followers | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Nano | ~1k–10k | Cheap UGC, high trust, product seeding |
| Micro | ~10k–100k | Best balance of reach, cost and engagement |
| Mid | ~100k–500k | Reach with credibility |
| Macro+ | 500k+ | Big launches, awareness; expensive |
Most small brands start with many nano and micro creators rather than one big name.

## Finding creators

- Platform creator marketplaces and affiliate programs (for example TikTok's creator tools and TikTok Shop affiliates)
- Hashtag and keyword search for the problem the product solves
- Creators already posting about competitors or the category
- Existing customers who post about the product

## Vetting checklist

- Audience location: a large share in the target country
- Engagement that looks real (comments with substance, not only emojis); watch for sudden follower spikes
- Content quality and on-camera comfort
- Brand safety: past content and controversies
- Past sponsored posts: did they perform, are they disclosed properly?

## Offer types

- **Gifting**: free product, no guaranteed post (best for nano creators)
- **Flat fee**: guaranteed deliverables
- **Affiliate / commission**: pay on sales; good for long-term partners
- **Hybrid**: smaller fee plus commission
- **Paid usage**: extra fee to use the content in ads (Spark Ads codes, partnership ads, whitelisting)

## Outreach message

Short, personal and specific: why this creator (mention a real post), what the product does, what you offer, one clear question. Send through the platform's creator tools, DMs or business email. Follow up once or twice, then move on.

## Agreement essentials

Deliverables and deadlines, review process, usage rights (platforms, duration, paid ads), exclusivity if any, payment terms, and disclosure requirements.

## Disclosure

US FTC rules require clear disclosure of material connections (paid, gifted, affiliate). Creators should use platform branded-content or paid-partnership labels and clear words such as #ad. Never ask creators to hide the relationship.

## Tracking

Give each creator a unique discount code and/or UTM link. Track cost per post, views, clicks, sales, and how the content performs when reused in ads.

## Output format

1. Creator criteria and tier mix with budget
2. Search plan (where and what keywords)
3. Vetting checklist
4. Outreach message templates (first message and follow-up)
5. Deal structure and agreement checklist
6. Tracking sheet columns
