# Conflict Hooks & Full Script Example

## Conflict hook templates (first 3 seconds)

| Conflict type | Visual | Spoken line template |
|---|---|---|
| Person vs problem | The old solution failing on camera | "Third time this [week/month] [problem happened]." |
| Person vs person | Someone pushing back on the purchase | "You spent HOW much on a [product]?" |
| Expectation vs reality | Something looks wrong, then flips | "This looks like a mistake. It isn't." |
| Stakes + clock | Visible deadline or countdown | "[Event] in [time] and [problem]." |
| Self vs past self | Old habit vs new result | "Me last month vs me now." |
| Creator vs comment | Green-screen a skeptical comment | "'[Doubt].' Okay, let's test it." |
| Rule broken | Doing what you're "not supposed to" | "I'm not supposed to [action] with this. Watch." |

Test: remove the conflict. If the hook still works the same, there was no conflict. Rewrite.

## Full script example (fictional CoreSeat, 30 s, TikTok/Reels)

**Concept**: Partner vs purchase. **Angle**: COMP + DEMO.

| Time | Visual / shot | Spoken line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Creator unboxing at desk; partner leans into frame, arms crossed | Partner: "You bought ANOTHER cushion?" | "3rd cushion this year 😩" |
| 3–7 s | Creator holds up an old flat foam pad, presses it flat with one finger | "Because this one died in a month." | "$30… flat in 4 weeks" |
| 7–15 s | Close-up: hand presses CoreSeat, foam slowly rises back | "This one's different. Watch it come back." | "High-density memory foam" |
| 15–22 s | Creator sits, leans back, relaxed; quick cut to 6 pm clock | "It's 6 pm and I'm not doing the stand-up groan." | "Still comfy at 6 pm" |
| 22–27 s | Partner sits on it, raises eyebrows | Partner: "…Okay. Fine. It's good." | "Partner approved ✅" |
| 27–30 s | Product on chair, creator points at link | "30 days to try it. Link's below." | "30-day comfort guarantee" |

**Five alternative conflict hooks for the same body**
1. Old pad pressed flat in one second: "I paid $30 for THIS?"
2. Creator groaning standing up: "This is me at 3 pm every single day."
3. Green-screen comment: "'Cushions don't fix anything.' Hold my coffee."
4. Car dashboard clock: "Four-hour drive and my back's done at hour one."
5. "Me in March vs me now" split screen: slouching vs upright.

## Creator brief (filled example)

- **Product & key message**: CoreSeat doesn't go flat; comfortable at the end of the day
- **Talking points**: your real sitting routine; what happened with older cushions; how it feels after a full day
- **Must show**: foam pressed and recovering; sitting on it at a desk or in a car; the cutout
- **Do not**: claim it cures pain or medical conditions; mention competitor brands; use unlicensed music
- **Deliverables**: 2 videos, 20–40 s, 9:16, raw footage + 3 alternative hooks each, no burned-in captions on one version
- **Usage rights**: paid ads for 90 days; Spark Ads authorization code for each post
- **Disclosure**: use the platform's paid-partnership label and #ad
- **Timeline / payment**: drafts in 7 days; payment within 7 days of approval
