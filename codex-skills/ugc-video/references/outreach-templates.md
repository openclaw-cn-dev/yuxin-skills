# Creator Outreach Templates

Fictional brand: **CoreSeat** seat cushion.

## First message (DM or email)

> Hi {name}! Loved your video on {specific video topic}, especially {specific detail}. 
>
> I run CoreSeat, a memory-foam seat cushion for people who sit all day. I think it'd fit your {desk setup / road trip / WFH} content.
>
> Could I send you one to try, no strings attached? If you like it, we'd love to talk about a paid video.
>
> — Aron, CoreSeat

## Paid offer message

> Hi {name}, thanks for trying CoreSeat! We'd love to work together on {number} video(s).
>
> - Deliverables: {number} × 20–40 s vertical videos, plus 3 alternative opening hooks each
> - Fee: ${amount} per video{, plus {x}% commission via your code}
> - Usage: we'd like to run the videos as ads for 90 days (Spark Ads / partnership ads)
> - Timeline: drafts within 7 days of agreement
>
> Does that work for you?

## Follow-up (once, after 4–5 days)

> Hi {name}, just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to adjust the format if something else works better for you.

## Agreement checklist

- [ ] Deliverables, length, format, number of hook variants
- [ ] Draft review: one round of edits included
- [ ] Posting date (if posting organically)
- [ ] Usage rights: platforms, duration, paid ads, authorization codes
- [ ] Exclusivity (if any): category and duration
- [ ] Payment amount, method, timing; commission terms
- [ ] Disclosure: paid-partnership label and #ad required
- [ ] Claims to avoid (medical claims, competitor names)
